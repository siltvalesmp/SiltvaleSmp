The History
of Siltvale.

HISTORICAL RECORD
AGE I

BEFORE THE SILT

The Age of Discovery

Eons ago, during what descendants would eventually call the Renaissance period, the world stood on the brink of an age defined by knowledge and machinery.

The enlightenment shaped the lives of countless peoples. New discoveries were being made, and technology was beginning to move the world toward a future built upon machinery.

That momentum would never reach its conclusion.

AGE II

THE PLAGUE

The Silt

The Silt Plague spread across the world. Gray and faintly fine beneath the sun, the Silt moved across continents and swallowed the homes of different races.

It brought tides of disease with it. Biomes withered, settlements disappeared, and the vibrance of the world slowly faded beneath the spreading Silt.

NATURAL RECORD / SILT

Pale Moss

Pale moss became one of the remnants associated with the Silt. Life continued to grow within the ruins of the old world, intertwining with abandoned structures and forgotten machinery.

AGE III

THE CREAKING

The Siltwraiths

The Silt did not merely consume lives. Over decades, it acquired enough consciousness — enough residue from those it had claimed — to become something new.

From the drifting haze emerged creatures unlike anything the world had previously known.

“Never turn your back.” — AN OLD WARNING PASSED BETWEEN SURVIVORS
“Don't look away.” — THE CREAKING
✮
ENTITY RECORD

The Creaking

Siltwraiths became the beginning of the apocalypse. They emerged from the haze as the world was already being consumed, turning the remains of the dead against the living.

AGE IV

THE FALL

The End of Siltvale

The Silt Plague and the apocalypse of the Siltwraiths eventually consumed Siltvale.

The survivors had little choice. They fled the choking Silt and scattered across distant islands.

Those scattered populations began forging a thalassocracy — bound together by the ocean and by a shared history that could not be forgotten.

The homeland was lost, but its history travelled with those who survived.

AGE V

GENERATIONS LATER

The Reclaiming

Generations passed.

The descendants of those populations who survived eventually turned their attention back toward the mainland.

“The Plague has ended and the Siltwraiths have become nocturnal. The mainland — our homeland — is to be reclaimed.”

Word spread across the islands. The homeland that had been abandoned was no longer unreachable.

The descendants would return to Siltvale and attempt to reclaim what had been lost.

AGE VI

THE RETURN

What Remains

The Siltwraiths still thrive at night. Their existence raises questions that remain unanswered.

QUESTION / 001

Residue

Are the Siltwraiths simply residue left behind by death, or is there something more within them?

QUESTION / 002

Behavior

Do they possess patterns, behaviors, communication, or even emotions?

Whatever the answer may be, the Siltwraiths remain a reminder of the lives lost during the Plague.

AGE VII

THE NEW WORLD

A Shattered World

The reclaiming of Siltvale is not simply a battle against monsters. It is a struggle to survive within a world that no longer resembles the one remembered by history.

Machinery lies abandoned and overgrown, remnants of knowledge from an age that existed before the Plague.

Descendants rediscover these machines and technologies through knowledge passed down between generations.

Nature has grown over the remains of civilization. Ruins, machinery, moss, and forgotten technology now exist together.
